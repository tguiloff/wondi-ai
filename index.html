The Default Mode Network (DMN)

The brain network that activates when your mind wanders

Altered DMN activity has been linked to anxiety, depression, and ADHD. fMRI studies show that mindfulness practice can reduce DMN activity and improve emotional regulation, focus, and cognitive resilience. — Brewer et al., PNAS, 2011
